The one-way delay for a message between Mars and a doctor on Earth.
The problem

Astronaut health alarms still use fixed, hospital-style limits.

False alarmsAstronauts exercise about 2 hours a day. Every workout trips a fixed heart-rate limit, so crews learn to ignore the alarms.
Late warningsA fever building, slow dehydration, a failing CO₂ scrubber: all stay "in range" until the problem is serious.
Our solution

ASCLEPIUS: the doctor on board.

How it works

Personal normal → early warning → action now.

01
LearnEach astronaut's own expected heart rate, oxygen, temperature and more, for sleep, work, exercise and spacewalks.
02
Detect & explainIt flags deviations early and shows why in plain numbers, then ranks the likely cause: hypoxia, heat stress, infection, arrhythmia, dehydration, CO₂.
03
Act nowThe crew gets the onboard protocol immediately. An Earth consult is queued, with the reply time adjusted for light delay.
